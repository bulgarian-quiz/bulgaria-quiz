# Visual / Mobile

Screenshots are in `../screenshots/` (desktop: home, games, about, flashcards; mobile: home).

- The homepage opens with the full-screen "Bulgaria" film. Above the fold on mobile is the wordmark plus the Skip and film controls. There is no textual statement of what the site is until the visitor scrolls the whole film or presses Skip.
- The navigation, games and flashcards pages render cleanly at 390 px with no horizontal overflow (checked earlier in this session) and 44 px touch targets.
- **Medium:** add a one-line Bulgarian intro ("Безплатни игри и флашкарти за историята и географията на България") visible in the first screen. It helps both users and crawlers.
