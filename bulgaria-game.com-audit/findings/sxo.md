# Search Experience (SXO)

Likely queries: "куиз области България", "игра карта България области", "флашкарти история на България", "тест по история 5 клас".

- These queries are usually answered by **quiz and test pages with visible questions** and **lists or tables of the 28 provinces**. This site's pages show a setup dialog or a cinematic intro instead, which is a page-type mismatch for the informational part of the intent.
- **Persona, student (11–14):** wants to start a quiz fast. The /games CTA works, but on the homepage the film delays the value (Skip helps).
- **Persona, teacher:** wants to know what it covers and whether it is free and safe. Nothing states the curriculum fit, cost or source of the facts.
- **Recommendation:** pre-rendered landing pages per intent, for example a page per game with a short description, 3–5 sample questions as text and a "Start" CTA, plus a 28-province reference table.
